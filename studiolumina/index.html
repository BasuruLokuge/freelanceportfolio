Our Story

Crafting Visual
Narratives

Studio Lumina is a Colombo-based photography studio specialising in architectural, commercial, and editorial imagery. We transform moments into timeless visual stories — from the dramatic skyline of Colombo to intimate portrait sessions that reveal the essence of a subject.

Every frame we capture is a considered composition of light, shadow, and emotion. Our approach blends technical precision with an artistic eye honed over years of working across Sri Lanka and the wider South Asian region.
